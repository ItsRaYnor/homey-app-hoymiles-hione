Bring your Hoymiles HiOne home battery into Homey and let it work together with the rest of your home. Follow its charge, the power going to and from the grid and what your house uses, all of it in Homey Energy as well. A price plan for the Dutch day-ahead market tells your Flows when energy is cheap enough to store and dear enough to use or sell, so the battery buys low and powers your home when it counts, also alongside other home batteries.

The app talks to the battery on your own network and keeps running when the internet is down. This is an unofficial app, not made or supported by Hoymiles.

This app is for the HiOne with its DTS-WL-G3 stick, and it controls the battery as well as showing it. Do you have Hoymiles microinverters with a DTU (DTU-WLite, DTU-Pro or DTU-Pro-S)? Then use the app Hoymiles Wi-Fi Solar Panel by Gabriel Pedrosa Machado, which reads those systems locally. The two apps work side by side.
